# Valerio Palangio

**Independent technology research and development in Italy.**

I lead [Palangio Enterprise](https://palangioenterprise.com), an independent initiative exploring AI-assisted analytical systems, data quality and provenance, and reliable software infrastructure.

## Current research

**AlphaSentinel** is a research-stage project exploring auditable, read-only NFT intelligence, with an emphasis on evidence, provenance, explicit limits, and human oversight.

The NFT research includes local, offline tools for structured evidence review, deterministic reports generated from synthetic examples, and verification of selected report packets. These capabilities support reproducibility and make missing information explicit; they do not establish the reliability of live market data.

The work includes a **private Browser Lab** for comparing crypto and NFT scenarios on synthetic data, with structured, deterministic explanations. This prototype remains separate from the public demonstrations and from live market analysis.
